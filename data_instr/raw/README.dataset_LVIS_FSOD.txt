# Introduction
This dataset is used for recognizing common objects in context. It includes 50 classes: Aerosol Can, Aquarium, Atomizer, Barrette, Bathrobe, Bib, Bulletin Board, Business Card, Camcorder, Cape, Casserole, Coleslaw, Costume, Cream Pitcher, Cub (Animal), Hair Curler, Diaper, Easel, Eraser, Fig (Fruit), Flute Glass, Gargoyle, Giant Panda, Grater, Grizzly, Heater, Jeep, Kitten, Mat (Gym Equipment), Monkey, Pew (Church Bench), Pinecone, Poncho, Postbox (Public), Potholder, Robe, Router (Computer Equipment), Saddlebag, Saucepan, Silo, Soupspoon, Mop, Telephone Booth, Tiara, Tiger, Tortilla, Vacuum Cleaner, Automatic Washer, Water Cooler, and Wig. The aim is to annotate instances of these objects in everyday environments.

# Object Classes

## Aerosol Can
### Description
A dispenser that holds a substance under pressure and releases it as a fine spray.

### Instructions
- Annotate the cylindrical body and the spray nozzle/cap. Include the cap even if it is translucent.

## Aquarium
### Description
A transparent tank of water in which fish and other water creatures and plants are kept.

### Instructions
- Annotate the outer glass frame. Do not include external stands unless they are integrated parts of the tank unit.

## Atomizer
### Description
A device for emitting water, perfume, or other liquids as a fine spray.

### Instructions
- Box the entire bottle and the bulb or spray mechanism used to dispense the liquid.

## Barrette
### Description
A clip or bar for holding hair in place.

### Instructions
- Box the visible decorative or functional part of the clip in the hair.

## Bathrobe
### Description
A loose-fitting robe, often made of absorbent fabric, worn before or after a bath or as loungewear.

### Instructions
- Capture the full extent of the garment on the person. Include sleeves and the hemline in the bounding box.

## Bib
### Description
A piece of cloth or plastic fastened round a child's neck to keep its clothes clean while eating.

### Instructions
- Annotate the front panel and the neck straps if they are clearly visible.

## Bulletin Board
### Description
A board for pinning notices or advertisements.

### Instructions
- Annotate the outer frame of the board. Include all items pinned to it within the box.

## Business Card
### Description
A small card printed with a person's name and professional information.

### Instructions
- Annotate the rectangular edges of the card. If being held, do not include the person's fingers.

## Camcorder
### Description
A portable electronic device for recording video and audio.

### Instructions
- Include the lens, body, and any flip-out screens or external microphones.

## Cape
### Description
A sleeveless cloak which fastens at the neck and hangs loosely from the shoulders.

### Instructions
- Annotate the full sweep of the fabric. Ensure the box covers the lowest point of the cape's hem.

## Casserole
### Description
A large, deep dish used both for baking and as a serving vessel, or the food cooked within it.

### Instructions
- Box the dish including its handles. If the lid is on, include the lid and its knob.

## Coleslaw
### Description
A side dish consisting primarily of finely shredded raw cabbage with a salad dressing.

### Instructions
- Annotate the mass of the food item itself within its container. Focus the box on the visible cabbage mixture.

## Costume
### Description
A set of clothes worn by an actor or performer, or for a particular activity or historical period.

### Instructions
- Identify the ensemble as a whole. The box should encompass all distinct elements of the outfit on the subject.

## Cream Pitcher
### Description
A small container with a handle and spout used for serving cream or milk.

### Instructions
- Include the spout and the handle in the bounding box. Ensure the base is fully captured.

## Cub (Animal)
### Description
The young of certain carnivorous mammals such as lions, bears, or foxes.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire young animal. Ensure the box is scaled to its smaller size relative to adults.

## Hair Curler
### Description
A device, such as a roller or heated iron, used for curling hair.

### Instructions
- Capture the individual roller or the barrel and handle of a curling iron.

## Diaper
### Description
An absorbent garment worn by a baby or person who is incontinent.

### Instructions
- Annotate the visible portion of the diaper. If worn under clothes, do not annotate unless visible.

## Easel
### Description
A self-supporting wooden or metal frame used by artists to hold a canvas or drawing board.

### Instructions
- Include the entire frame, legs, and any central support beam. If a canvas is on it, include the canvas within the box.

## Eraser
### Description
An item used for removing marks from paper or a board (e.g., pencil eraser or chalkboard eraser).

### Instructions
- Box the entire object. If it is a small pencil-top eraser, ensure the box is tightly fitted to the rubber material.

## Fig (Fruit)
### Description
A soft pear-shaped fruit with sweet dark flesh and many small seeds.

### Instructions
- Annotate the fruit body. Include the small stem if it is still attached to the fruit.

## Flute Glass
### Description
A tall, narrow glass used for serving sparkling wine.

### Instructions
- Include the base, the thin stem, and the bowl of the glass in the bounding box.

## Gargoyle
### Description
A carved or formed grotesque with a spout, designed to convey water from a roof.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire stone carving. Include the wings and head in the bounding box.

## Giant Panda
### Description
A large bear-like mammal with distinctive black and white markings, native to China.

### Instructions
- Annotate the full body. Pay special attention to capturing the ears and limbs in the box.

## Grater
### Description
A kitchen tool with a rough, perforated surface used for shredding food like cheese or vegetables.

### Instructions
- Box the entire tool including the handle. Capture the object regardless of whether it is currently being held or in use.

## Grizzly
### Description
A large North American brown bear known for its size and powerful build.

### Instructions
- Box the entire bear. Ensure the hump on the shoulders and all limbs are within the annotation.

## Heater
### Description
A device for warming the air in a room or building.

### Instructions
- Annotate the exterior casing. Include visible heating elements or grilles.

## Jeep
### Description
A sturdy, high-clearance vehicle designed for off-road driving.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire vehicle body, including spare tires mounted on the back and any roof racks.

## Kitten
### Description
A young domesticated cat.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire small feline. Use tight bounding boxes to exclude as much background as possible.

## Mat (Gym Equipment)
### Description
A padded floor covering used for gymnastics, wrestling, or general exercise to prevent injury.

### Instructions
- Annotate the visible boundaries of the mat on the floor. If folded, capture the folded silhouette.

## Monkey
### Description
A small to medium-sized primate, typically having a long tail and climbing trees.

### Instructions
- Include the full body and the tail. If the monkey is hanging, ensure the box extends to the tail's tip.

## Pew (Church Bench)
### Description
A long bench with a back, placed in rows in a church.

### Instructions
- Annotate the individual bench unit. If benches are connected, try to isolate a single row.

## Pinecone
### Description
The conical woody fruit of a pine tree.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire cone. Ensure the box captures any protruding scales.

## Poncho
### Description
A garment made of a single sheet of fabric with an opening for the head, worn as a raincoat or wrap.

### Instructions
- Annotate the outer boundaries of the fabric as it hangs from the wearer. Ensure the box covers the full drape.

## Postbox (Public)
### Description
A large metal box in a public place where letters can be dropped for collection.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire structure from the base to the top slot/opening.

## Potholder
### Description
A piece of quilted or thick fabric used for handling hot kitchen pots.

### Instructions
- Box the entire fabric square or glove. If being used, include the hand only if it is inside a mitt-style holder.

## Robe
### Description
A long, loose outer garment reaching the ankles, often used for ceremonial or official occasions.

### Instructions
- Annotate the full vertical length of the garment from shoulders to hem.

## Router (Computer Equipment)
### Description
A networking device that forwards data packets between computer networks.

### Instructions
- Include the main chassis and any external antennas. Ensure antennas are fully contained in the box.

## Saddlebag
### Description
A bag attached to a saddle or positioned over the back wheel of a bicycle or motorcycle.

### Instructions
- Include the bag itself and any visible straps or buckles used for attachment.

## Saucepan
### Description
A deep cooking pan, typically round, made of metal, and with one long handle and a lid.

### Instructions
- Annotate the pan body and the handle. Include the lid handle if the lid is present.

## Silo
### Description
A tall tower or pit on a farm used to store grain.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire vertical structure from the ground to the conical roof.

## Soupspoon
### Description
A large spoon with a round bowl used for eating soup.

### Instructions
- Include the entire length of the handle and the circular bowl of the spoon.

## Mop
### Description
A tool consisting of a bundle of thick strings or a sponge attached to a long handle for cleaning floors.

### Instructions
- Include both the cleaning head and the full length of the handle in the bounding box.

## Telephone Booth
### Description
A small structure furnished with a public telephone.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire structural exterior. If the door is open, the box should extend to cover the door's edge.

## Tiara
### Description
A jeweled ornamental band worn on the front of a woman's hair or as a crown.

### Instructions
- Include the full width and height of the ornament. Ensure the box accounts for any high-reaching jewels or peaks.

## Tiger
### Description
A large carnivorous feline with a distinctive coat of dark vertical stripes on orange-brown fur.

### Instructions
- Annotate the full body from head to the tip of the tail. If the tail is extended, the box must include it.

## Tortilla
### Description
A thin, flat maize or wheat flour pancake, eaten hot or cold, typically with a filling.

### Instructions
- Annotate the flat surface. If rolled (like a taco/burrito), box the entire cylindrical shape.

## Vacuum Cleaner
### Description
An electrical apparatus that by means of suction collects dust and small particles from floors.

### Instructions
- Annotate the main unit, the hose, and the cleaning head as one object if connected.

## Automatic Washer
### Description
A household appliance used for washing laundry automatically.

### Instructions
- Annotate the entire exterior housing of the machine. Include the control panel and door/lid.

## Water Cooler
### Description
A device that cools and dispenses drinking water, typically from a large inverted bottle.

### Instructions
- Annotate the stand and the water bottle on top. Include the dispensing taps.

## Wig
### Description
A covering for the head made of real or artificial hair.

### Instructions
- Annotate the hairpiece itself. Try to exclude the wearer's forehead or neck where possible.
